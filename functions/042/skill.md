# Skill description — 042 (marketing copy drafting)

- **Purpose**: Draft public-facing marketing copy (web, LinkedIn, deck
  snippets, email) grounded in Canvas Intelligence's frozen positioning
  brief (`docs/positioning.md`) — the messaging house, five competency
  pillars, named product pillars (Sage, Powerfleet, BuildSmart), and CFO
  voice-of-customer language embedded in `prompt.md`.
- **When to invoke**: Any task asking for new or revised public-facing
  copy about Canvas Intelligence's positioning, offering, or product
  pillars — homepage copy, LinkedIn posts, case-study framing, pitch
  snippets.
- **When NOT to invoke**: Internal-only documents, sales proposals with
  their own approved templates, or any content that would restate a
  client name or sales-deck-sourced claim without first confirming a
  permission-register entry (see `prompt.md`'s hard constraint) — route
  that check through the Brand Steward QA function
  (`functions/brand-steward-qa/`) before publishing.

- **Tools available**: see `tools.yaml`
- **Evaluation**: see `evals/`
