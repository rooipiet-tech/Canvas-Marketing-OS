statement: A remediation CI step written specifically to fix one deploy-wiring blocker can itself mask failures if it swallows all failure modes identically and never re-verifies its own effect.

evidence: A CI step added specifically to fix a blocker (orchestrator's client base-URL env vars never wired) redirects az CLI stderr to /dev/null with `|| true`, treating genuine auth/IAM failures identically to the benign "resource not yet deployed" case it exists to tolerate, with no read-back step confirming the env vars actually landed on the target Container App — the workflow can report success while having silently skipped the wiring it exists to perform.

catch_next_time: Any CI/deploy step added specifically to remediate a "live resource state didn't match what the app needs" bug must (1) distinguish the specific benign failure mode it exists to tolerate from all other CLI failure modes, hard-failing on the latter instead of a blanket `|| true`, and (2) include a read-back/verification step confirming its own effect actually landed before the job reports success. Treat "a fix step swallows its own failure and never re-verifies its effect" as a standing review-lens check whenever a patch touches deploy/CI wiring, not just application code.
