statement: GOAL text arriving with generic placeholder paths/service names that
don't match this repo's actual established directory conventions needs an explicit,
negotiated resolution before spec-writer freezes touch-scope — and resolving it at
that stage (rather than leaving it for the builder to guess mid-build) is what makes
it a clean, non-blocking negotiation instead of a rework cycle.

evidence: In the s1-gateway build, the GOAL referred to "the gateway"/"/services/
gateway" generically, while the repo's actual scaffold was `services/model-gateway`
with a frozen `contracts/model-gateway/openapi.yaml`. This was caught and resolved
via an explicit "locked decisions" negotiation in the research stage, documented and
frozen before spec-writer ran — avoiding any mid-build ambiguity about which paths
were actually in scope.

catch_next_time: At the research/domain-expert stage, before spec-writer starts,
explicitly diff every path/service-name the GOAL text names against a real
directory listing of the repo. Any mismatch becomes a locked decision (via
AskUserQuestion or an explicit research-brief entry) resolved before the spec
freezes — never leave a generic-vs-real-path ambiguity for the builder to guess at.
