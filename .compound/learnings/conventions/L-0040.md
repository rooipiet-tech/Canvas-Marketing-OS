statement: A plan's coverage_check table mapping "step N -> criterion X" is necessary but not sufficient evidence of coverage — it does not confirm the step's action text actually produces every concrete sub-case the criterion's own verify text names (e.g. a specific fixture, value, or scenario).
evidence: In the s6-registry build, plan v1's coverage_check table listed a step against AC-17 (permission-register gating), but AC-17's verify text named a specific required test case — a client name entirely absent from the register, proving "absent == UNCLEARED" — that no step actually produced. The plan-reviewer caught this as blocker F2 despite the table technically claiming coverage; plan v2 step 7 added the explicit fabricated-client-name assertion.
catch_next_time: During plan review, for each criterion whose verify text names a specific concrete scenario/fixture/value (not just a category or general capability), search the plan's step action-text for that literal scenario; if it is absent, raise a coverage-gap finding even when the coverage_check table lists a step against that criterion.
