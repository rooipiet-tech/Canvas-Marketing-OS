statement: An advisory-only domain constraint that a domain-expert correctly
declined to freeze as a Must-have (because the frozen GOAL text never mentioned
it) can still surface post-build through review. When multiple independent review
lenses converge on the exact same concrete defect and the fix is cheap and mirrors
an already-proven pattern in the same build, that convergence is itself strong
evidence to promote the finding to a new Should-tier acceptance criterion and
patch it immediately, rather than recording it as a dangling non-blocking note.

evidence: output-reviewer (F-02), risk-security (RISK-01), and data-residency
(DR-01) all independently found the same gap: none of 12 packages'
prompt.md/tool_check.py had a personal-data/named-individual guardrail, despite
fn11's taxonomy inviting a hiring/leadership-change person-name and fn13's Method
section directing LinkedIn scans. Zero of the 41 frozen ACs mentioned
personal/PII/individual (confirmed by grep) because the frozen GOAL text never
raised it — domain.md's C-7 had flagged it only as advisory. The orchestrator
added AC-42 (Should) and routed a small builder patch (mirroring the proven
no-client-naming pattern) rather than leaving it unaddressed.

catch_next_time: Orchestrator/reviewer-triage — when >=2 independently-run review
lenses land on the identical finding (same file/pattern), treat the convergence
count itself as elevated signal regardless of individual severity ratings;
cross-check domain.md for a matching advisory-tier constraint that never became a
Must-have, and if the fix is a small mirror of an already-enforced pattern, add a
new Should-tier AC and patch now instead of deferring to a docs-only follow-up.

strengthened_2026-08-02 (session/s9-analytics): sharpens the convergence principle
into one concrete, mechanically greppable pre-check. performance-lens (PERF-2) and
risk-security (RISK-01) independently converged on the identical defect:
vault_client.py hardcoded limit=500 with no pagination across 4 getters
(get_costs/get_agent_runs/get_assets/get_campaigns), while contracts/vault-api.yaml
defines both Limit and Offset parameters on all 9 of its list endpoints — a
defined-but-unused pagination param in the contract is itself the smell. Fixed in
v2 via a shared _get_paginated() helper looping on offset until a short page
returns; re-verified resolved by both lenses. New pre-check to fold into
plan-review/build, to catch this BEFORE two lenses have to independently
rediscover it: whenever a client hardcodes `limit=N` against a contract-defined
list endpoint, grep that endpoint's contract definition for an Offset/cursor/page
parameter; if defined but unused in the client, flag it immediately as a
pagination gap.
