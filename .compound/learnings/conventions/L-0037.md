statement: Spec acceptance-criteria verify text that references review process/role/artifact labels (e.g. a "security/agent-native/general-output" checklist) should use the loop's own defined reviewer/lens vocabulary rather than inventing parallel ad hoc labels — otherwise a substantively-satisfied criterion can fail purely on literal-label mismatch.
evidence: In the s6-registry build, the only tester failure (AC-25, minor) was exactly this: the frozen criterion demanded a "security/agent-native/general-output" 3-way checklist, but the loop's actual taxonomy is output-reviewer + risk-security + fired lenses (agent-native, data-residency in this run). Substantive review intent was fully met per review.json/risk.json/lenses.json, yet the literal artifact-naming match failed.
catch_next_time: When drafting a criterion whose verify text names review roles or process artifacts, check those names against the loop's actual reviewer/lens vocabulary (output-reviewer, risk-security, lenses.json's lens names) and reuse those exact names, or phrase the criterion around substantive coverage rather than a named checklist artifact.
