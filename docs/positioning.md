# Canvas Intelligence — Marketing Positioning Brief
*Prepared 24 July 2026 · Input for the Canvas Marketing OS*
*Sources: Sales Deck 2025 (Sage), Delta Finalist Presentation, Sage Solution Architecture, Gert/Hestony Powerfleet deck, Delta References, canvasintelligence.com, LinkedIn page review, CFO pre-meeting survey*

---

## 1. The problem this brief solves

The sales and proposal material is dramatically stronger than the marketing layer. The Delta finalist deck sells a CA-led, Fabric-native, reference-backed enterprise platform with hard SLAs and a USD 300k fixed price. The website sells generic "fast, visual reporting" — with lorem ipsum still in the Support section and no mention of Fabric, the Chartered Accountant DNA, the productised platforms, or a single named client. The key competencies exist; they just never made it into the marketing.

**The core insight: Canvas doesn't sell dashboards. It sells *trusted numbers* — and it's one of very few firms structurally built to make that claim.**

---

## 2. Recommended core positioning

> **Canvas Intelligence is the Chartered Accountant–founded data engineering firm that turns multi-ERP chaos into one governed source of truth — financial reporting reconciled to your audited numbers, delivered on Microsoft Fabric in weeks, not years.**

Short forms:

- **Tagline (keep):** *Your Data. Delivered.* — it's established, distinctive and on-brand.
- **Positioning line for web/LinkedIn:** *One source of truth across every entity, ERP and currency — built by accountants, engineered on Microsoft Fabric.*
- **Elevator pitch:** *Since 2013 we've built enterprise data platforms on the Microsoft stack for groups like Imperial Logistics, ArcelorMittal, Weir and Rotork. Founded and led by CAs, we deliver financial report packs that reconcile to your audited consolidation — plus sales, operations and payroll analytics — from pre-built platforms that go live in weeks. Beyond the dashboard: your data finds you.*

## 3. The five competency pillars (with proof)

These are the competencies that repeat across every document but are invisible in current marketing.

### Pillar 1 — Finance-grade trust: "numbers you can take to the board"
The single strongest differentiator. No generic BI shop can say this.
- Founded and led by Chartered Accountants; JP van Zyl CA(SA) as engagement sponsor (Delta deck).
- Financial Report Packs "reconciled to the audited group consolidation… designed and reviewed by Chartered Accountants" (Delta deck).
- Commits in writing: 99.5%+ reconciliation to source, a reconciliation variance treated as Severity-1 (Delta deck).
- Directly answers the market's loudest pain — the CFO survey: "finance, operations and commercial will have a different number for the same question"; "numbers conflict depending on who you ask… everywhere."

### Pillar 2 — Multi-entity, multi-ERP, multi-currency consolidation at scale
The repeatable, referenced "hard problem" Canvas solves.
- Imperial Logistics: 40+ business units, 14+ ERP systems (SAP ECC6, SAP B1, Oracle, Sage X3/200/Evo, AX, Great Plains, Pastel and more) consolidated into one Azure lakehouse, HFM group financials traceable to ERP transactions (Delta references).
- Delta proposal: 8 entities, 3 countries, 4 currencies (ZiG/USD/ZAR/ZMW), 2 fiscal calendars, SAP S/4HANA plus 6 operational systems — one governed group view without losing local statutory truth.
- FTSE-250 and blue-chip references on file: Weir, Rotork, ArcelorMittal SA, SGB Cape, Imperial.

### Pillar 3 — Microsoft-native depth, Fabric-forward
- Microsoft stack since 2013; Gold Partner for Data Analytics since 2018; now Solutions Partner for Data & AI; ECIF partner (Sales Deck 2024/2025).
- Live Microsoft Fabric implementations in production (industrial services group live since July 2025; Rotork Fabric implementation; Dynamics/Dataverse Fabric Link client), medallion architecture, Direct Lake at 4TB scale (Delta deck).
- Proven migration story: Synapse → Fabric "without breaking a single downstream dashboard" (Delta deck).
- Marketing angle: *Fabric is the fastest-growing conversation in the Microsoft data world — Canvas has production proof while most competitors have slideware.*

### Pillar 4 — Productised platforms: weeks, not years
The "product stack" repositions Canvas from services firm to platform company.
- **Sage Cloud Analytics Platform** — turnkey DaaS: on-prem agent → cloud medallion → pre-built Power BI semantic model + REST APIs; wizard setup, first value in a day (Sage decks).
- **Powerfleet / logistics platform** — driver behaviour, fatigue risk, turnaround times, live KPI alerts, cost-per-km, integrated with Sage 300 finance and payroll cubes (Gert deck).
- Pre-built cubes: Sage 300 Finance, People/Payroll; Canvas for BuildSmart (construction); ~34 "Canvas for X" product lockups already exist in the brand system.
- Commercial model to match: "simple and predictable pricing", fixed-price scoped delivery (Delta: USD 300k fixed, milestone-tied), packaged engagement for sub-10M-row clients.

### Pillar 5 — Beyond the dashboard: management by exception + AI
The forward-looking story that earns attention.
- "Let your data find you" — exception management, momentum-based alerts, email subscriptions, live KPI monitoring (Sales Deck 2024, Gert deck).
- Copilot grounded on governed semantic models; plain-English questions on trusted data (Delta deck).
- Skills transfer and CoEaaS as deliverables — certification sponsorship, embedded client staff, code in the client's Git tenant, "built to be owned by you" (Delta deck, references).

## 4. Who we're talking to (and what they feel)

Primary buyer: **the office of the CFO in multi-entity groups** (South Africa + Southern Africa, expanding to UK/US via Weir/Rotork-type references). The CFO survey gives the exact voice-of-customer language marketing should mirror:

- "More than 3 days" a month on reporting, cleaning data, reconciling systems, Excel manipulation.
- "No more Excel accounting."
- "Different number for the same question" across finance, ops and commercial.
- Waiting on trial balances, intercompany matrices, consols.

Secondary audiences: COO/operations leaders in logistics and manufacturing (Powerfleet story), CIOs mid-SAP/ERP migration (the "platform survives your ERP cut-over" story), and Sage-ecosystem mid-market (productised platform).

Vertical proof already in hand: logistics & distribution (Imperial, Hestony/Powerfleet), mining & industrial (ArcelorMittal, Weir, Rotork), beverage/FMCG (Delta), construction (BuildSmart), financial services (Fabric client).

## 6. What to focus on (priority order)

1. **Fix the shop window first.** The website actively undermines every deck: lorem ipsum in Support, template contact details (per the LinkedIn review: fake New York address), no Fabric, no CA story, no clients, no products. Rebuild the homepage around the positioning statement and five pillars before spending on any demand generation.
2. **Publish the proof.** Turn the five Delta references and the finalist-deck case studies into public case-study pages / LinkedIn proof posts (one metric each). The LinkedIn review reached the same conclusion: replace platitude posts with proof posts. Get written permission where references are currently "on request".
3. **Lead with the CA + reconciliation claim everywhere.** It's unique, defensible, and maps to the #1 buyer pain. Make "designed and reviewed by Chartered Accountants" a standing badge on web, LinkedIn, decks and proposals.
4. **Productise the naming.** "Canvas for Sage", "Canvas for Logistics (Powerfleet)", "Canvas for BuildSmart" — one product family page, consistent lockups (the brand system already has 34). This shifts perception from bespoke consulting to scalable platform.
5. **Own the Fabric conversation locally.** Content series: Synapse→Fabric migration story, Direct Lake at 4TB, medallion in production. Aim: "the Fabric partner with production proof" in the SA market.
6. **Feed the Marketing OS.** Load this brief as the Tier-2 (Strategy & Positioning) source of truth: value proposition matrix = Section 3, audience/pain matrix = Section 4, messaging house = Section 5, content priorities = Section 6.2–6.5.
